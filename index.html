      How to order

      1. Study the plate. It’s the outline of one of New Jersey’s 564 municipalities: every city, town, borough, township and village. North is up, and the bar under the plate shows the scale.
      2. Order a town. Start typing and pick from the list. Duplicate names like the six Washington Townships are labelled by county.
      3. Read the check. Each miss tells you how many miles off you are, which way to head, and how close you are (100% is a bullseye).
      4. Ask your server. After three misses your server tips you off to the county. After five you also get the town type and population.
